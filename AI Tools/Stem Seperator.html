How The Separation Works

1

Upload Audio Track

Drop your MP3, WAV, or FLAC track into the integrated studio panel above.

2

Configure Quality Mode

Choose Fast & High Quality (HTDemucs) for quick rendering, or Slow & Best Quality (MDX-Extra) for maximum vocal-instrument separation.

3

Demucs AI Inference

The audio stream is processed via deep-learning spectrogram analysis, splitting the track into 4 distinct stem layers (Vocals, Drums, Bass, Other).

4

Stream & Download

Preview each isolated stem directly inside the terminal, then save high-quality WAV or MP3 files to your local system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the live web integration work?
The web app panel above communicates directly with our cloud GPU backend. It processes audio tracks live without leaving your browser or requiring local file downloads.
What is the difference between HTDemucs and MDX-Extra?
HTDemucs is optimized for fast execution (~1 minute per track) with balanced frequency isolation. MDX-Extra takes slightly longer to render but delivers maximum isolation quality on dense EDM and rock arrangements.
Is there any file size or duration limit?
Because the separation runs on dedicated cloud hardware, you can process full-length tracks (up to 10+ minutes) without running into browser memory limits.
