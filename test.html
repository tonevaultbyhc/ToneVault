Rhythm Keyword Guide

PATTERN BEHAVIOR

drivingStronger, more continuous pulse
syncopatedMore off-beat and anticipatory hits
four-on-floorKick on every quarter note
sparseFewer notes and more breathing room
busyMore subdivisions, ghost notes and fills
rollingRepeating fast subdivisions / rolls

INDIAN RHYTHM

tablaUses Indian tabla-style phrasing
teen taal16-matra classical cycle
kuthuParai-led South Indian folk / dance pulse
dholLow/high folk percussion emphasis
tihaiThree-part phrase resolving to the downbeat

CINEMATIC / ORCHESTRAL

cinematicLarge orchestral percussion palette
epicHeavy low hits and strong impacts
tensionRising subdivisions and restrained hits
buildIncreasing density toward the phrase ending
taikoLarge low cinematic drum character
